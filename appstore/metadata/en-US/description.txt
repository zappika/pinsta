Keep the places you come across in one place.

Share a public post or a place link to Vicolo. It finds the place and saves it to a map on your iPhone. If a post names several places, choose the ones you want to keep. If Vicolo is unsure, pick the right place from its suggestions.

Your map brings together restaurants, cafés, bars, shops, museums and more. Browse by city or kind, mark somewhere as Been there, and open directions when it is time to go.

You can also paste a link, search by place name, or bring in a shared Google Maps list. Vicolo works with public posts from Instagram and TikTok, and with Google Maps place links.

No account is needed. Your places are stored on this iPhone; Vicolo does not sync them between devices.
