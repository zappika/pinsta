Vicolo saves places to a personal map on the iPhone. A person shares or pastes a link to a place they came across (a Google Maps place, or a public post that names a venue), Vicolo finds the place, and it appears on their map. They browse by city or kind, check in when they are there (with their own photos and a note), and open directions. No account or sign-in is needed, and the list stays on the device.

HOW TO TEST (no social app needed)
1. A map link: tap +, choose Google Maps, paste https://www.google.com/maps/place/Cal+Pep/@41.3810,2.1810,17z/data=!3d41.3810!4d2.1810 . Cal Pep in Barcelona is found and saved.
2. A public post: tap +, choose Instagram post, paste https://www.instagram.com/p/DdJIacKIplU/ . The caption mentions the venue; tap Save next to Ästad Vingård.
3. A post naming several places: paste https://www.instagram.com/p/C_OOWiDS7Gh/ in the same field. The places are read from the post's pictures on the device; keep the ones you want and tap Save.
4. A shared list: tap +, choose Google Maps, paste https://maps.app.goo.gl/2LvBeE5dXwo2iQcz6 to import it.
5. At a place: the "I'm here" button above the map lists places around you and checks in to one.
Safari → Share → Vicolo opens the same save flow for any supported link.

LOCATION
Used to open the map on the town the person is in and for "I'm here". It is asked only after two places are saved, after an explanation screen. The device location never leaves the phone.

THIRD-PARTY CONTENT
Vicolo reads only the single public post the person chose to share, the same information a link preview shows: the caption, the account name and the post's picture. From these it works out which place the post is about. The picture is kept as the person's reminder of where they saw the place, always next to a button that opens the original post in its own app. For a post that names several places, each place keeps the picture its name appeared on. There is no feed, no browsing of anyone's posts, and no way to download, export or reshare media. Places saved from map links, typed names or check-ins carry no third-party content at all.

The app also offers native MapKit browsing, a share extension, grouping by city and kind, check-ins with the person's own photos, and on-device text reading (Vision) for multi-place posts.
