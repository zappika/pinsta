Vicolo asset exports

cities/: 21 latest city illustrations, with city captions excluded. Objects named in one or two words. index.csv maps each object to its city.
objects/: 51 original collection objects and groups, including the expanded set. Connected accessories stay together (the motel key includes its melon-soda charm).
These are native-resolution transparent PNG cutouts from approved posters. Original artwork pixels are preserved; macOS Vision generated alpha masks with manual mask repairs for the grey whippet, rice ball, palace arch, Berlin tile panel, Madrid frame and coffee steam. These are not upscaled or regenerated masters. The baked lighting and glass reflections remain part of the original artwork. source-crops/ preserves the opaque originals and is not published by the library build. The two whippet noses are intentionally edge-cropped in the source. Use the contact sheets to browse.

menu-icons/: 11 separately generated simplified transparent category assets. Each has a full-resolution master and 24/48/72px exports for a 24pt menu at 1x/2x/3x. Restaurant uses gilda; Other uses coral camera, distinct from mint shopping bag. Built-in image generation was used; master generation brief: one centered rounded 3D collectible, transparent background, bold simplified silhouette for 24px, category-specific distinct color, no text or tile.
VicoloMenu.xcassets/: matching Xcode image sets. Render using original colors, not template tinting.

The backoffice library serves the transparent versions. Earlier posters and source-crops remain unchanged.
